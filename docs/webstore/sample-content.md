## Keep Useful Reading Notes in Markdown

Source: `https://example.com/reading-notes`
Author: Open Context Clipper Demo
Published: 2026-11-20

A good reading note captures the idea you want to revisit, not every sentence on the page. Start with a short summary in your own words. Add the source link, then list one or two questions the article raised for you.

Plain Markdown files make these notes easy to move between editors and folders. You can search them locally, back them up with your other documents, and keep reading without deciding on a permanent app first.

When a page is worth saving, export it directly or keep it in a queue until you have time to review it. A small, deliberate collection is often more useful than a large pile of untouched links.

## A Simple Weekly Review for Saved Articles

Source: `https://example.com/weekly-review`
Author: Open Context Clipper Demo
Published: 2026-11-19

Set aside a few minutes each week to look through the articles you saved. Open the ones that still matter, add a brief note about why you kept them, and export the useful ones to your own folder.

You do not need a complex system. The goal is to turn a saved page into a file you can find and use later.
